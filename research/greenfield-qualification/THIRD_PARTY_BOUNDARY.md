# Third-Party Boundary — Current Qualification State

No production Rules Core is frozen and no private production repository has
been created.

The qualification workflows may fetch the exact external pins, apply a
research-only patch in an ephemeral checkout, compile, and record evidence.
They must not copy the old Commander-Lab Rules Core into this project. The
current Forge patch is limited to the typed decision-boundary experiment and
does not establish a production license or interop decision.

The process boundary, modified-source obligations, generated artifacts,
runtime dependencies, and legal uncertainties must be revisited after a
candidate passes all Q0–Q8 gates. Until then the safe classification is
`RESEARCH_ONLY_NOT_FOR_DISTRIBUTION`.
